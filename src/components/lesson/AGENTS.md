# Lesson screen rules
- The intro and every lesson step use the one `TutorChatPanel` (side panel from tablet up, bottom strip + sheet on phones); lesson stages never render their own tutor bubble. Close and minimise both collapse it into `CollapsedTutor` (state per session in sessionStorage, badge/preview logic in `src/shared/tutor-collapse.ts`). Why: one chat-safe tutor surface.
- Quick fix logic: `src/server/lesson/quick-fix.ts`; its content and step answers live only in hidden `scripts`, sent one step at a time without answers. Why: answers never reach the browser early.
- Video step (`src/components/lesson/VideoStage.tsx`): player, captions, segments and checks come from the clip row (title, duration_s, segments, captions, urls); seek limits, evidence strength and chat moves live in `src/shared/video.ts`. Why: the real video arrives as a manifest change only.
- Simulation step: the band's `simulations` row picks the engine (`url` = `gradient-lab-v2` (unchanged copy in `GradientLabV2Stage.tsx`, level via `src/shared/lab-v2.ts`, its `complete` report settles the step as lab_reported), `gradient-lab` or `native:*`, `init.lab` = stages/missions, `init.fallback_engine` = built-in board). v2 gets outside-only touches in `lab-v2-reader.ts` `dressV2` (hide its logo, tap outside closes "Leave"), and our Ask Fiti hides while v2 is on screen. Gradient Lab runs in `GradientLabStage.tsx` (same-origin frame, contract 1.0); no "ready" within rule `sim_load_timeout_ms`, an error, or low-data switches the session to the fallback (`simFallback`, logged). Lab tasks are `sim_tasks` whose hidden check has `engine: "gradient-lab"`; every result is re-marked by `src/server/maths/gradient-lab-checks.ts`, never by the lab's `passed`. Why: changing a record changes what loads, and the lab never decides.
- Worked examples play through `WorkingPlayer` (one typing pace from rules `chat_stream_chars_per_s`, `working_line_hold_ms`, `working_result_pause_ms` in `src/shared/working-pace.ts`, shared with streaming chat bubbles in `StreamingText.tsx`; mirrors lines into one chat block); quick fix step dialogs come from server state `fix.dialogs` and close via `repairDialogFn`. Why: one pacing/chat model, and dialogs survive a reload.
- The tutor picture everywhere in the lesson uses `src/components/tutor/LiveTutor.tsx` (CSS motion; rule `tutor_avatar_assets` can swap in animated files). Why: one place to restyle or replace the avatar.
- The warm-up (readiness) step renders inside `mcq/WarmupShell.tsx` (bare Fiti shell: logo, Dark mode and name only; Hint/Ask Fiti; thinking parrot with question mark, no speech bubble). Ask Fiti opens a clean chat for the question on screen (approved `warmup_ask_open` line, then only messages from her first ask on this question) as a left card on desktop and a bottom sheet on phones. Hint tips must pass `isClearTip` (`src/server/lesson/hint-reply.ts`), else the approved step tip, then question tip, plays. Hint asks for the approved tip through the normal ask path and shows it in the tip pill, once per question. Warm-up answers use `.ans-pill` tokens; numbers in the question use `splitNumbers` (`src/shared/question-numbers.ts`) and the `--q-num` colour. Why: one multiple-choice template reused by the quick fix and later video checks.
- The whole quick fix (watch + your turn) plays in `whiteboard/WhiteboardDialog.tsx` over the unchanged question page: a white card with a plain white panel (no background shapes) and orange edge (no board/marker/eraser pictures — never bring them back), our animated Fiti (clips follow `whiteboard/clip-controller.ts`, tested), and Gradient Lab-style motion rebuilt as our own `qf-*` CSS. Tips follow the lab: Fiti's bubble holds the question, he wears the fallback line `qf_tap_hint` as a pill while a step's hint is unseen, and hops/glows every rule `qf_idle_hint_ms` of stillness (`src/shared/idle-hint.ts`, tested), and tapping Fiti opens `HintCard` with only the step's approved hint from `repairHintFn` (never a try); a right step plays `qfBurst` + `GotItCard` (title = fallback `qf_got_it`) from `whiteboard/QfCards.tsx`. Why: one look reused from the simulation, and hints stay server-owned.
- The hook step renders inside `mcq/WarmupShell.tsx` with `noHint` (Ask Fiti only); the hill picture, roads and cyclist are code (`HookStage.tsx`), nudge/skip timing is `hookTimers` in `hook-board.ts` (tested), and all its lines are hook content or approved fallbacks. Why: one shell for question-style steps, and the prediction is never marked.
- Background music: two produced loops (Lovable assets `src/assets/music/`) played by one player `src/lib/music.ts`, rules (moods per page/step, silence, ducking) in `src/shared/music-plan.ts` (tested); pages only set `data-lesson-step`, `__root` picks the mood. Why: one place for every music rule.
- Celebrations go through `celebrate.tsx` `useReaction()` only (small / big / notQuite), with sounds in `src/lib/sound.ts`. Why: every checked answer reacts the same way.
- Practise (worksheet) renders in `mcq/WarmupShell` with `PractiseStage.tsx`: quick-fix pieces from `qf-shared.tsx` and `whiteboard/*` (Fiti clips portalled into the shell's `#practise-fiti` slot, tap-for-hint pill/nudge via `idle-hint.ts`; hints drop in as a bubble beside Fiti (never the chat or a modal), GotItCard; keypad from `src/shared/practise-keys.ts`, tested); server logic unchanged, her working is restored from `view.workLines`. Why: one look with the quick fix and simulation, same rules.
- Fiti reads every worked-example line aloud from `WhiteboardDialog` (say on write; next line waits for `voiceIdle()` up to a ceiling; hush on close/pause/Watch again); maths symbols become words only in speech via `src/shared/spoken-form.ts` (tested). Why: voice and board stay together and the board never stalls.
- Video step with real clips: clip row `flow` (checks: after/correct/wrong[3]/next clip ids + media urls) switches `VideoStage` to `src/features/video-lesson/FlowVideoStage.tsx`; clip order is pure `flow.ts` (tested); no flow = the placeholder timer clip. Dave's copied pieces are recorded in its `SOURCE.md`. Why: real clips arrive as content, and marking stays on the server.
